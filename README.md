🕵️ About the Project

Info Detective is an AI-powered web application inspired by the fun and mischievous world of Shinchan. It transforms ordinary information searching into an interactive investigation experience.

The platform allows users to ask questions, explore predefined topics and discover information through AI-generated responses.

Whether it's science, technology, history, general knowledge or something unexpected, Info Detective encourages users to stay curious and investigate beyond the obvious.

🎯 Problem Statement

Finding reliable and meaningful information online can sometimes be overwhelming. Users often encounter scattered information, lengthy explanations and difficulty identifying relevant answers.

Info Detective aims to simplify information discovery through an interactive, AI-powered platform that makes exploring questions more engaging and accessible.

💡 Our Solution

We bring AI-powered information exploration into a single, user-friendly detective-themed platform.

Users can:
- Enter their own questions.
- Explore predefined questions.
- Discover information through different categories.
- Receive AI-generated responses.
- Explore topics through an engaging interface.
